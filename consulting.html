Case
Nike APP User Experience Research & CX Consulting
Background
Concentrix Solution
Value
The client aimed to assess the real-life experience of its official Nike APP. Concentrix delivered user experience research across multiple business scenarios to identify critical APP pain points, support Nike's business decisions, and boost user satisfaction & brand loyalty.

We combined qualitative and quantitative methods with usability testing as the core, providing end-to-end service from user recruitment to report delivery in 7 phases:

  • Design evaluation tasks for 10+ scenarios
  • Establish issue type and severity grading standards
  • Recruit users by age, location, shopping preference, new/returning user tags
  • Deliver full training for test moderators
  • Conduct one-on-one user testing, collect usability issues, completion rate and task time data
  • Classify and grade issues, create issue lists with screenshots
  • Finalize experience evaluation report
  • Comprehensive insight: Cover 11 user journeys, provide a full issue list for APP optimization
  • Clear prioritization: Help Nike focus on high-impact areas to deploy resources efficiently
  • Actionable reference: Directly enable follow-up experience upgrades and business decisions
  • Experience to loyalty: Resolve key pain points, lift APP experience and strengthen user satisfaction & brand loyalty
