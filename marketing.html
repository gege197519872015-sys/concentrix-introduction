Renowned Chinese Restaurant Chain rapidly penetrates overseas with advanced omnichannel marketing tactics

Background
Concentrix Solution
Value

V* is a rising brand of China-based and global-renowned restaurant group H*, established in NA and targeting to grow into more global markets.

Based on the initial setup of digital infra such as POS, KIOSK, and third-party food delivery, the client has a deep need to enhance their digital marketing capabilities and focus on "enlarged channel coverage" and "refined operations for growth."

Provide a set of comprehensive, efficient, and secure digital marketing and commerce solutions spanning in multiple channels and platforms including Web, App, EDM, Social Media, Membership Mall, Marketing Automation tools.

Continuous marketing operation services:

  • One-ID and data integration for omni-channel operations
  • Campaign Planning & Execution
  • AI Powered Persona & Ads Attribution Analysis for Conversion Funnel Optimization
  • SEO & GoogleAds Management
  • Experience-centric: Build a digital marketing matrix aligned with local culture, and seamless end-to-end user journey across omnichannel touchpoints, cultivate highly engaged loyal brand users.
  • Conversion-prioritized: Establish a dual-drive engine of "public domain traffic acquisition + private domain long-term operation". Leverage audience segmentation and lifecycle-based engagement to lift overall conversion rates and average order value (AOV).
  • Retention-oriented: Implement foundational data-driven analytics frameworks. Gain deep insights of consumer behaviors and preferences to inform targeted marketing outreach, maximize repeat purchases and extend customer lifetime value (LTV).
